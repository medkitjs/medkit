---
title: YouTube and Vimeo facades
description: Show a local thumbnail and a play button, and load the provider's player only when the visitor presses play.

---

An embedded YouTube or Vimeo player loads the provider's script and sets its cookies before
anyone presses play, for a video most visitors never start. A facade shows a thumbnail instead, and loads the player
on click.

```sh
pnpm exec medkit thumbnail youtube:dQw4w9WgXcQ
```

```js
renderPlayer({ provider: 'youtube', id: 'dQw4w9WgXcQ', title: 'A talk', poster: youtubeManifest }, { popup: true });
```

| Moment | What loads |
| --- | --- |
| Page load | The local thumbnail only, fetched and processed at build time |
| Hover or focus | A preconnect to the provider |
| Click | The provider's iframe, autoplaying, from `youtube-nocookie.com` for YouTube, focused, titled |
| No JavaScript | A link to the video around the thumbnail |

YouTube's `maxresdefault` thumbnail is tried first, then `hqdefault`, whose letterbox bars
are cropped out. Vimeo's comes from its oEmbed API.

## What it does not do

- Once loaded, the provider's own controls and overlays show; medkit's control bar does not
  apply to them.
- In a popup, closing removes the iframe, which is the only way to stop a cross-origin player
  without its API, so reopening starts the video over.
