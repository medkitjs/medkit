`renderVideoBg(clips, options)` renders `<medkit-video-bg>` from each clip's video and poster manifests, a title and an optional overlay, and works with no JavaScript. `VideoBg` components for React and Astro render the same bytes. Astro components are now imported as `@medkitjs/elements/astro/Image` and `@medkitjs/elements/astro/VideoBg`.
